SRFSK8 MUNICH — TERMS & PRIVACY

1. Terms of Use & Disclaimer

SRFSK8 Munich is an independent, non-commercial 3D web game and interactive simulation created as a creative tribute to surfskate culture and the concrete flow bowl "Im Gefilde" in Munich, Germany.

All gameplay mechanics, speeds, air times, and maneuvers are simulated for virtual entertainment. Real-world skateboarding and surfskating involve inherent physical hazards. Always wear proper protective equipment (certified helmet, knee/elbow pads, wrist guards) and follow local skatepark safety rules in the real world.

2. Privacy Policy & Vercel Web Analytics

We value your privacy and believe in data minimalism:

  • Privacy-Friendly Analytics: This site uses Vercel Web Analytics to monitor anonymized visitor metrics (e.g. page views, country, device type) and track virtual game maneuvers to improve performance and gameplay balance.
  • No Tracking Cookies: Vercel Web Analytics does not use cookies, does not store persistent identifiers across websites, and does not collect personally identifiable information (PII). It complies fully with GDPR, CCPA, and PECR.
  • No Account Required: No registration, login, email address, or payment data is required to play.
  • Local Storage: Your control preferences (Arrow Keys vs WASD) and graphics quality setting are saved strictly in your local browser (localStorage) and are never transmitted to external servers.

3. Credits, Assets & Intellectual Property

Skatepark terrain geometry is reconstructed from public LiDAR elevation data and drone photogrammetry of Skatepark "Im Gefilde", Munich. Original soundtrack and audio clips are used with artist permission or under royalty-free creative licenses for non-commercial web use.

Feedback, trick suggestions, or skatepark requests are welcome on Instagram: @surfskate_munich.
